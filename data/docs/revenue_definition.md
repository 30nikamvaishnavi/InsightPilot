# Revenue Definition

## Revenue formula
Revenue is calculated for each order line as quantity multiplied by unit_price multiplied by one minus discount. A quantity of 2, a unit_price of 1000 and a discount of 0.10 gives revenue of 1800.

## What revenue excludes
Revenue excludes taxes and shipping charges. Refunds from returns are not subtracted from the revenue column, so net sales after returns must be calculated by subtracting refund_amount separately.

## Revenue recognition timing
A sale is counted as revenue on the order date, not on the delivery date or payment date.
