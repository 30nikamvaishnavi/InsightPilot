# Regions

## Region definitions
The business reports four regions. North covers Delhi, Punjab, Haryana and Uttar Pradesh. South covers Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Telangana. East covers West Bengal, Odisha, Bihar and Assam. West covers Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa and Rajasthan.

## Region reporting
Each region has a regional manager who owns the quarterly target and reviews the monthly sales report. Regional managers report to the head of sales.
