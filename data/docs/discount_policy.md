# Discount Policy

## Discount limits
The maximum discount that can be applied to any order line is 25 percent, stored as 0.25 in the discount column. Standard orders usually carry a discount between zero and 20 percent.

## Approval rules
Any discount above 15 percent needs written approval from the regional manager before the order is confirmed. Discounts of 15 percent or less can be given by the sales representative.

## Seasonal promotions
During festival sales such as Diwali, the discount limit can be raised temporarily by the head of sales. The promotion dates and limits are announced by email and apply only to that campaign.
