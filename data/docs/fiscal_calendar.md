# Fiscal Calendar

## Fiscal year
The fiscal year follows the calendar year and runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## Quarters
Q1 covers January to March, Q2 covers April to June, Q3 covers July to September and Q4 covers October to December.

## Reporting cadence
The monthly books are closed by the fifth business day of the next month. Quarterly reviews are held in the second week after a quarter ends.
