# KPI Glossary

## Average order value (AOV)
AOV is total revenue divided by the number of orders in the same period. It shows how much a typical order is worth.

## Month over month growth
Month over month growth is the percentage change in a metric compared with the previous month, calculated as the current month minus the previous month, divided by the previous month.

## Return rate
Return rate is the number of returned orders divided by the number of orders sold, shown as a percentage. It shows what share of orders get sent back.

## Units sold
Units sold is the sum of the quantity column for the selected period or filter.
